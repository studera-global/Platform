SAT, ACT & MDCAT Mentorship

Receiving guidance from Instagram reels or short YouTube videos are nothing more than dopamine boosters. They lack consistency, practice, and mislead students — becoming one of the greatest barriers to achieving a 1480+ on the SAT, a top score on the ACT, or a competitive MDCAT result.

No more impractical Instagram influencers giving useless training for a top score. Studera is bringing real guidance from people who have actually scored 1480+ on the SAT, aced the ACT, and topped the MDCAT — and will walk you through their exact preparation path and mentorship strategy.
