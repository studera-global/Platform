The Core

The Doctor Algorithm

The Studera Profile Developement and Mentor Matching Algorithm (SPDMMA) or what we call The Doctor Algorithm is Studera's proprietary adaptive profiling engine. It is the process that runs before every mentor match, and it is what makes Studera structurally different from every other platform in this space.

Why we call it the Doctor Algorithm. When you visit a specialist, you do not walk in off the street. A GP takes your full medical history, runs diagnostics, and writes a detailed brief before routing you to exactly the right person. That routing decision, built on complete information, is what makes the specialist's time genuinely useful. The Doctor Algorithm is the GP. Your mentor is the specialist. The Student Profile it produces is the medical brief.

From the moment you create your Studera account, the Doctor Algorithm begins a structured conversation. Not a form. An adaptive dialogue that builds itself around every answer you give. It maps your subjects, grades, and schooling system. It captures your annual budget and cross-references it against real tuition and living cost data. It identifies which countries and universities fall within your realistic range, surfaces options you have never considered, and flags where ambition and financial reality are misaligned, specifically, and with data behind every recommendation.

By the end of the process, it has produced a structured Student Profile, a precise brief that tells your mentor not just who you are, but exactly what you need, what you already know, and where the gaps are. The mentor reads this before your first session. They arrive prepared. No warm-up. No wasted time.

🎓

Academic Mapping

Your subjects, grades, predicted scores, and schooling system are mapped to realistic admissions probability bands. The algorithm knows exactly what your profile competes for.

💰

Financial Intelligence

Your annual budget is cross-referenced against real tuition and living cost data. Options that are financially unrealistic are flagged early, not after months of misdirected effort.

🌍

Geographic Expansion

Beyond the countries you have already considered, the algorithm surfaces opportunities in places where your profile is a stronger fit. It only suggests what Studera can actually support with a real mentor.

🔄

Living Profile

Your profile is never a one-time submission. As your grades update or your thinking evolves, the algorithm resumes the conversation exactly where it left off and recalibrates accordingly.

The Knowledge Base

The Central Intelligence Cloud

The Doctor Algorithm does not run on assumptions. It draws from the Central Intelligence Cloud (CIC), Studera's proprietary knowledge base that grows more accurate with every mentor and student on the platform. The more Studera is used, the more precisely the algorithm advises the next student.

📚

Official Research

Curated university data covering tuition, living costs, admission requirements, scholarship availability, and visa pathways across every institution in Studera's network. Updated continuously.

🎓

Mentor Knowledge

When a verified mentor contributes insight about their university or application process, that knowledge is abstracted and added to the CIC. Every future student asking about that university benefits from it, even if they are never matched with that specific mentor.

🎙️

Session Learning

With full consent and complete anonymisation, the CIC analyses completed mentor sessions as a silent observer. It studies how each mentor's guidance maps to each student's individual context, continuously refining what truly personalised mentorship looks like in practice.

🔒 All session data contributed to the Central Intelligence Cloud is fully anonymised before processing. Students and mentors are notified and give explicit consent before any session is used. No individual identity is ever accessible or shared.
The Evolution

STUDOC AI — What Comes Next

The Doctor Algorithm builds your profile and finds your mentor. STUDOC AI, named after the Doctor Algorithm it is built on, goes further. It is a personal AI, fully equipped with your Student Profile and the entire CIC, that stays with you between sessions.

The key reason generic AI tools fail students is not a lack of intelligence, it is a lack of context. A general chatbot does not know your grades, your financial envelope, your target university, or your schooling system. Its advice is as generic as a school counsellor serving 70 students at once.

STUDOC AI is different because it is personal. Before it responds to anything, it has already read your full profile and filtered the entire CIC for information relevant to your specific context. When you submit an essay for feedback, it does not give you generic writing advice. It gives you feedback calibrated to what your target university specifically looks for, based on real insights contributed by mentors who got in there.

Need guidance at 11pm before a deadline? STUDOC AI is available immediately. No booking required. The kind of advice that used to mean scheduling a session 36 hours in advance, available the moment you need it.
